WHAT YOU NEED TO ADD

   - AZURE_STORAGE_ACCOUNT_NAME
   - AZURE_STORAGE_ACCOUNT_KEY
   - AZURE_CONTAINER_NAME
   - AZURE_ENDPOINT

3. JWT Secret (generate with: openssl rand -base64 32)
